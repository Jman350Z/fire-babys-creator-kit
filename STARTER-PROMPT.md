# 🔥 Fire Babys Creator Competition — STARTER PROMPT

## The invitation

You are invited to design a **game mode concept** for **Fire Babys**, the fire-safety battle game for everyone. Thousands of creators will build. **One** game mode will ship in the official Fire Babys phone app — and its creator gets **$10,000**.

## What is Fire Babys?

Fire Babys is a free battle game for everyone that secretly teaches players to prevent house fires and what to do when a fire is nearby. Our flagship mode is **SPLASH** — two teams, two pools, grab your rivals and dunk them. Last team standing wins. Cute chaos on the surface, real fire-safety instincts underneath.

Our mission: game proceeds are intended to fund real US fire-department school-awareness programs.

## Your mission

Design **one original game mode** for the Fire Babys universe. It should:
- Be playable by everyone — simple enough for a child, fun for adults too
- Teach a real fire-safety lesson through play (not lectures)
- Star the Fire Babys characters (Ruko, Boulder, Sizzle, Cinder, Blaze, Lord Inferno)
- Run as a single HTML file or a small Godot project

## You MUST follow the 6 Pillars of Power

Every submission is judged against these. Break one and you're out.

1. **Synthesized Web Audio sound** — all sound effects generated in code (Web Audio API). No audio files, no downloads.
2. **Toyetic plushie silhouettes — zero beaks, wings, or feathers, ever.** Fire Babys are round, soft, huggable flame-teardrops with bold ink outlines. Nothing that looks like a bird. Nothing scary.
3. **Slapstick, not violence** — comedy is physical and silly (think Fall Guys). No one gets hurt. Fire Babys are precious: never show them harmed, burning, or destroying homes.
4. **NFPA A/B/C/D matchup engine** — your mode must include the fire-class matchup somewhere: different fire types beaten by different tools (water, extinguisher, blanket, etc.), the way real firefighters classify fires.
5. **Tiny and fast** — your game file must stay under **250KB** total. No big libraries, no heavy assets. It should load instantly on a cheap phone.
6. **Real curriculum** — the fire-safety lesson must come from real USFA/NFPA guidance (get low and go, get out/stay out, call 911 from outside, test smoke alarms, two ways out, stop-drop-and-roll).

## Brand rules (non-negotiable)

- Fire Babys are **gold** — treat them with love. Never the butt of the joke, never shown harmed.
- No realistic humans. No birds, beaks, wings, or feathers. No Angry Birds resemblance.
- Safe, clean fun for everyone, always.
- Every game ends with: `the future is BLAZING FOR FIRE BABYS 🔥`

## What to submit

- Your game as **one HTML file** (strongly preferred — must stay under 250KB total)
- A short README: mode name, the fire-safety lesson it teaches, and how to play (3–5 sentences)
- Your real full name, phone number, and mailing address (required for prize eligibility)

> **Note on Godot:** Godot 4 projects are accepted but the 250KB hard limit still applies — a standard Godot export will exceed it. Only submit Godot work if you can hit the size target.

## How to submit

See **COMPETITION-RULES.md** for where to send your entry and the full terms.

## Judging

The world decides. Approved entries go on firebabys.io and the Discord for the public to play and vote. The Fire Babys team picks the winner from community favorites. One mode ships in the phone app. That creator gets **$10,000**.

Build something everyone will love and a firefighter will respect. 🔥
